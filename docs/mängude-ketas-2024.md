MMXXIV is the year in which JUKU E5104 support
made it [officially into the MAME emulator](https://www.mamedev.org/?p=544).
Juku 3000 marks this by releasing the
JUKU games disc.

``2048    .COM`` - Puzzle game "2048" (Pehka1985 '22)  
``9       .COM`` - Puzzle game "9-VANGLA"  
``ATSKOO  .COM`` - Card game "Atskoo" (Kohtla-J 1KK)  
``AVTEST  .COM`` - Magic squares (=Sudoku, VSW '91)  
``BOWLING .COM`` - Bowling (Maxway & I.K.S. '91)  
``BUGABOO .COM`` - Turnip in the maze\* (EKTA '89)  
``CATCHUM .COM`` - Well-known CP/M PAC-MAN clone\* (80x24)  
``CHESS   .COM`` - Chess "Mõtlen & käin" 1.1 (T.Ainsaar?)  
``GAMEBOY .COM`` - Fly and shoot (A.Säde '91)  
``HITCH   .COM`` - Hitchhiker's guide (80x24, IC '85)  
``HUNTER  .COM`` - Hunting club (80x24, Vakstu/MIG '86)  
``INDY    .COM`` - Indy Looking for Jewels (I.Käär '91)  
``KAPTEN  .COM`` - "Battleship" (ed K.Koppel)  
``LADDER  .COM`` - Well-known CP/M platformer (80x24)  
``LAOLEO  .COM`` - "Laoleo" (=Sokoban, Kompuuter '91 H)  
``LOGER   .COM`` - Switch logic v1.1 (Nost Neji '90 H)  
``LOGEX1  .CLG`` - LOGER data file  
``MADUOK  .COM`` - Classic snake game (=Snake)  
``MODX    .COM`` - 80x24 screen-mode driver  
``MOIS    .COM`` - Manor lord's efficiency (=Sumerian)  
``MOND    .COM`` - Launch angle (=Artillery Duel)  
``PUSHER  .COM`` - "Pusher" (=Sokoban, A.Mett '91)  
``READ    .ME ­`` - The text you are reading now  
``SHOT    .COM`` - "Shot Master 2000" v1.1 (I.Käär '91)  
``SNAKE   .COM`` - "Snake"\* (M.Gladin/EKTA '87)  
``SPACE   .COM`` - "Space Attack" (Vakstu/VSW '90)  
``ZOO     .COM`` - "Lode Runner" at the zoo\* (Vakstu '91)  
``TANK    .COM`` - Tank vs. UFOs (=Sabotage/Invaders)  
``TET     .COM`` - "Tetris" with mouse (A.Mett '90 H)  
``TETRIS  .COM`` - Graphical "Tetris" (T.Ainsaar '90)  
``XONIX   .COM`` - "Xonix"\* (=Qix, E.Jürviste/EKTA '87)  

`*` - the game's controls have been modernised  
`H` - a mouse is required to play the game  
`80x24` - requires setting the screen mode via MODX  

The disc can be downloaded [here](https://elektroonikamuuseum.ee/failid/juku/tarkvara/J3KGAME1.JUK) and for now matches the web emulator's `B:` disc. Enjoy diving into the early history of Estonian computing!
