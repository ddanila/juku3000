in 2025, but most of them are ports. This
year's games disc is dedicated to better- and
lesser-known copies and new versions of
abandonware.

Most of the fresh ports have been done by the veteran Pehka1985, unless otherwise noted. The Stupidity demo and Hitchhiker's guide were ported by Tramm. The latter, and Ladder, require the 80x24 screen mode, which can be enabled with `MODX`. Ladder uses a separate font, and `*` marks games with emulator-friendly key bindings.

The disc can be downloaded [here](https://elektroonikamuuseum.ee/failid/juku/tarkvara/J3KGAME2.JUK) and for now matches the web emulator's `A:` disc. Have exciting adventures in the wondrous world of retro rallying!
